# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, appearance, ability or beliefs. Participation should be constructive, respectful and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good faith while addressing harmful impact directly.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety and community boundaries.
- Help make technical discussions accessible to contributors with different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal attacks, sustained disruption, deliberate intimidation, doxxing and retaliation are not permitted. Publishing exploits, credentials or private vulnerability details outside the process in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) is also unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community spaces and public interactions where someone represents the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through a contact method on their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue that identifies affected people. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as practical, disclose conflicts of interest and respond proportionally. Actions may include a private warning, content removal, temporary restriction or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. Knowingly false or manipulative reports may themselves violate this policy.
